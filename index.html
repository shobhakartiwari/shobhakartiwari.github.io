Values

How I approach the work.

What twelve years of shipping iOS apps, leading teams, and mentoring engineers has taught me to care about.

◎

Users first

Every feature starts with the person holding the device, whether it's a flight attendant mid-service or a customer paying a bill.

✦

Craft matters

Accessibility, performance, and polish are part of the job, not extras. Small details decide whether people trust an app.

▦

Build strong foundations

Clean architecture, solid tests, and steady modernization keep an app easy to change long after its first release.

∞

Collaborate without ego

The best idea wins, wherever it comes from. Good code reviews teach, and a strong lead helps the whole team grow.
